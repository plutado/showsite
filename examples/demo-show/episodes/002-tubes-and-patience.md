A listener mailed us a kitchen radio in a shoebox. Our guest Imelda
Thornquist, who restores radios in a converted garage, walks us through
bringing it back slowly and safely.

We talk about the **dim-bulb tester**, a simple series lamp that limits
current the first time an old set is powered up, and why patience is the
most important tool on the bench.
